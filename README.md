Bank Transfer UX Case Study

A practice product design project focused on improving the experience of sending a bank transfer.

The Problem

Some users start a bank transfer but don't complete it because they may:

- Not trust the recipient information
- Be unsure of the final amount and fees
- Not know whether the transfer went through
- Struggle to change something before sending
- Think cancelling a transfer means the money is already back

My Approach

I designed the flow around two key moments:

Before the transfer
Help the user feel confident about who they're sending to, how much they're sending, and what they're committing to.

After the transfer
Clearly show what happened to the money.

Key Screens

Select Recipient
Helps the user make sure they're sending the money to the right person.

Enter Amount
Helps the user confirm they're sending the right amount.

Review Transfer
Gives the user one last chance to confirm the recipient and amount before sending.

Transfer Status
Separates successful, pending, and failed states so the user knows what happened and what to do next.

Design Principle

I decided what information to show based on what the user needs to complete the task confidently.

What I Would Do Next

I would test the flow with real users and use their feedback to improve it.
